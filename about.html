ABOUT HOUSETHAT

Designing Beyond Ordinary

HouseThat is a design-led interior architecture and construction studio founded on a simple belief—exceptional spaces are never created by chance. They are thoughtfully imagined, meticulously detailed, and flawlessly executed.

Established in 2022, HouseThat has grown into a multidisciplinary design practice delivering bespoke residential, commercial, hospitality, and architectural projects across India and the UAE.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Spaces With Identity

Every project reflects our philosophy of timeless design, intelligent planning, and uncompromising attention to detail.

From luxury residences to boutique commercial environments, our work is defined not by a signature style, but by the people who inhabit the spaces we create.

We believe every project deserves its own identity—crafted around the client's lifestyle, aspirations, and vision.

OUR EXPERTISE

From Concept to Construction

Every project is handled as a complete journey—from the earliest ideas to the final handover. Our multidisciplinary approach allows us to design, build, and execute spaces with complete consistency and attention to detail.

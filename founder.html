FOUNDER

Anita Shah

Anita Shah is the Founder & Principal Architect of HouseThat, a multidisciplinary design studio creating timeless residential, commercial, hospitality and bespoke spaces across India and the UAE.

With a design philosophy rooted in simplicity, craftsmanship and purpose, her work blends contemporary luxury with spaces that remain deeply personal and enduring.

HER JOURNEY

Building HouseThat

In 2022, Anita founded HouseThat with a simple vision—to create a design practice where architecture, interiors, and execution work seamlessly together.

Today, HouseThat is a multidisciplinary design-build studio offering: 1. Architecture 2. Interior Design 3. Turnkey Execution 4. Space Planning 5. Residential Design 6. Commercial Design 7. Hospitality Projects 8. Project Management 9. Design Consultation 10. Bespoke Furniture 11. Interior Styling & Photography 12. 3D Visualisation Every project is approached with the same commitment to quality, transparency, and thoughtful execution—regardless of its scale.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Spaces That Feel Timeless

Anita believes that the role of an architect extends far beyond creating beautiful spaces. The true responsibility of a designer is to understand a client’s lifestyle, aspirations, traditions, and personality so deeply that the completed home feels as though it could never have belonged to anyone else.

Rather than designing around a signature style, Anita designs around people. Because the most meaningful homes are not the ones that follow trends—they are the ones that feel deeply personal.

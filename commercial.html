21st Century Rooftop Bar and restaurant

Location

Nashik, India

Year

2023

A beloved landmark in Nashik for over three decades, 21st Century has long been a favorite gathering place across generations. Our redesign respected its legacy while introducing a fresh identity that feels timeless and inviting.

The rooftop was reimagined as a warm, intimate, and character-filled space, with layered lighting, rich materials, and thoughtfully designed seating that encourages people to slow down, connect, and create memorable experiences. The result is a refreshed destination that preserves the soul of an iconic restaurant while elevating its atmosphere for today’s guests.

Komal Kumavat academy

Location

Nashik, India

Year

2022

A 600 sq. ft. makeup academy thoughtfully designed to blend education, beauty, and retail into one cohesive experience. The space accommodates makeup training for up to 25 students, professional vanity stations, and a striking entrance that reflects the brand’s identity.

Both balconies were transformed into functional extensions of the studio—one as a charming café and photography corner, and the other as a boutique featuring jewellery, clothing, and a changing room. Every corner was designed to maximize space while creating an inspiring environment for learning and creativity.
